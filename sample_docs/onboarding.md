# Onboarding Guide — Acme Robotics

## First week

Every new engineer gets a mentor and a starter project. The starter project
must be shipped to staging by the end of week two.

## Access

Request VPN and GitLab access through the IT portal. Production database
access requires a separate approval from the infrastructure lead.

## Deployment

We deploy via GitLab CI. Every merge to `main` triggers the staging pipeline;
production deploys happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays after a manual approval.

## Vacation policy

Employees get 28 paid vacation days per year. Requests go through the HR
portal at least two weeks in advance. Unused days do not carry over.
